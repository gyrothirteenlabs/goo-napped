## Full description

The alchemist took the kingdom. Now the slimes are being drained of their aura, and the strongest of them are locked in jars.

You are a quiet, clear-aura slime. Nobody could read your aura, so you were thrown out of the kingdom and left to survive. That clear body has one gift: it can take in essence and copy any aura you eat. Use it to split yourself, become the friend you need, and get them out of the laboratory before the guards catch you.

**How it plays**
- **Eat essences** to grow. The last essence you eat becomes your aura, and each essence has its own side effect: glowing, smelling, weighing you down, going cold, or slipping into the shadows.
- **Split** into two pieces. Put a piece into a friend's jar to make a copy that frees your friend. Pieces can merge again when they touch.
- **Shift** control between your pieces, or to a rescued friend waiting in the vents.
- **Hide** inside barrels, crates and other objects. You cannot move while hidden, and a guard who stares at you long enough will search the hiding spot.
- **Guards chase you** once they notice you. Break their line of sight and hide to lose them. Being caught sends you back to the last vent.
- **Vents** are your safe places, checkpoints, and exits. Rescued friends wait in a hideout between levels, and some of them can help from the vents with limited powers.

**Features**
- 11 levels across three areas (garden, dungeon and laboratory, castle) and the summit
- Eight friends to rescue, each with their own jar and recipe
- Seven essences with different side effects
- Guards and creatures with unique behaviors: a Witch Eye that checks hiding spots, a Painting that watches with its eyes, a Hollow Armor that hunts sound, and the Alchemist
- Story told through comic-strip cutscenes and dialogue
- Dark, moody pixel art and a lighting system where light is danger
- Keyboard controls, pause menu, options for music, sound, fullscreen and resolution

**Theme: SHIFT**
- Your body changes: you split, copy auras, and shrink or grow with essence.
- Your control changes: switch between pieces, or hand over to a friend.
- The guards' shifts change: a guard who is off duty leaves a position, and the watch changes hands.
- The world changes: the kingdom becomes a laboratory, and at the end the roles are reversed.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / Arrow keys | Move |
| E | Eat essence, use, interact |
| Space | Split / merge |
| Shift | Switch control |
| C | Hide / come out |
| F | Call or wait for a friend, use a friend's power in the vent |
| Q | Spit out the last essence |
| R | Retry from the last checkpoint |
| Esc | Pause |
